# Demo video — shot list

**Required for submission.** No video, no judging. Record it Saturday evening
while you can still think, not Sunday at 09:30.

Target: **90 seconds.** Judges watch a lot of these. Everything below is on
screen or cut.

---

## Before you hit record

- [ ] `npm run dev`, then **click Derive once** so the cache is warm. A cold run
      is three minutes and will not fit in the video.
- [ ] Have `/hire/[id]`, `/manager` and `/pay` already open in tabs.
- [ ] Browser zoom to ~125%. Judges may watch this on a laptop; default zoom
      renders your body text unreadably small in a compressed upload.
- [ ] Close Slack, mail, notifications. A notification banner mid-demo looks
      careless.
- [ ] Test your mic. Bad audio reads as low effort more than bad video does.

## The shots

**0:00–0:15 — the problem, over the landing page**

> "We went out on the street on Friday and asked people what was broken at
> work. The first two people we met worked at Legora. They hire fifty people a
> week, most of those roles have never existed at the company before, and
> nobody has time to look after anyone."

**0:15–0:30 — why nothing existing works**

> "So you can't write documentation, because the role is being invented while
> they hire for it. You can't use search, because the answer isn't written
> down. And you can't assign a mentor, because there isn't a spare one. The
> only thing left that scales is an agent."

**0:30–0:55 — the derived role** *(the money shot — spend the most time here)*

Scroll slowly through the role card, then stop on the evidence citations.

> "Nobody wrote this. It read three weeks of their Slack and worked out what
> the job is. And every single claim points back at the message it came from —
> if a quote isn't really in the source, we drop it."

Then stop on `openQuestions`:

> "And where the company genuinely hasn't decided something, it says so instead
> of inventing an answer."

**0:55–1:15 — real work, not a reading list**

Open Day 1, Task 1.

> "Day one, task one: a real client deliverable with a real deadline, with the
> context needed to actually do it. Not a reading list."

Then type a question into the chat and let the answer land.

**1:15–1:30 — the manager screen, and the close**

> "And the manager sees blockers. Not scores — deliberately. They hire for
> ownership, so we show what's in someone's way and who can clear it, and
> nothing about how well they're doing.
>
> We're not selling documents. We're selling back the supervision capacity that
> doesn't exist."

## Rules

- **Do not narrate the UI** ("here you can see a button"). Narrate the argument.
- **Do not run a live derivation.** Cached is honest — the response carries
  `cached` and `derivedAt`, and the UI says when it was derived.
- **One take is fine.** Slightly rough and confident beats polished and late.
- Show the real product. No mockups, no slides pretending to be product.

## After recording

- [ ] Upload to Loom / YouTube / Drive
- [ ] **Open the link in an incognito window.** Every year teams lose on a Drive
      link set to "restricted". If a judge can't open it, it does not exist.
- [ ] Paste into the submission form early — you can resubmit as many times as
      you like before 10:00, and the last version before the cutoff is judged.

## Two different videos — do not confuse them

**The submission video is a screen recording.** The form asks for "a short video
showing your product actually working". An animated explainer does not satisfy
that, and substituting one risks the requirement outright. Record it yourself,
this evening, from the live site.

**Visually's video is for distribution**, not submission. Nasa Dadkoush (founder,
Visually — at this hackathon) has offered to generate one for us manually. That
belongs on the LinkedIn posts, which are judged on *real distribution* and carry
20 rubric points plus two marketing prizes, and on the landing page. A
professionally animated 30 seconds will out-reach a text post by a wide margin.

**Never let a required deliverable depend on someone else's Saturday.** He is
building at the same hackathon. Treat his video as upside that may not arrive.
