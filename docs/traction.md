# Traction playbook — Saturday

Traction is 18 of 50 points. The bands:

| Points | What it takes |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Nothing |
| 6 | Waitlist, or several demo requests |
| 12 | **1 paying customer + signed LOIs** |
| 18 | **3+ paying customers** |

Everything below exists to move up that table. The build does not score. This does.

---

## The ask ladder

Never open with the LOI. Ask for money first — leading with the LOI turns a
possible customer into a definite non-customer. Walk down only when you have to:

1. **Pay now.** Open `/pay` on your phone, let them scan the QR. `/buy` skips
   every login wall and lands straight in Stripe Checkout. Forty seconds.
2. **LOI.** `/loi` on their phone. Ninety seconds, renders a signed statement
   with a server timestamp. Screenshot it *there and then*.
3. **Demo request / waitlist.** Worth 6 points and worth a follow-up. Get the
   WhatsApp number, not the email.

> **Stripe is still on test keys.** A test-card payment is correctly recorded as
> `livemode: false` and does not count. Swap to live keys **and live price IDs**
> before the first real sale — the price IDs are environment-specific and this
> will bite you at exactly the wrong moment.

## Capture everything in the moment

The submission takes up to 8 proof images. At 09:40 Sunday you will not be able
to reconstruct any of this.

- Screenshot every reply, every signed LOI, every Stripe payment.
- Photograph the meeting.
- Drop each one into a shared album as it happens.

A conversation that isn't captured scored zero, no matter how well it went.

## Saturday outreach

### 1. Legora — the warm lead

Joseph Cassidy is **People Enablement Partner / Senior Talent Partner** at
Legora — ex-Lovable, ex-Pleo. He is not a random contact, he is the buyer: his
own profile says "optimizing onboarding". He is a first-degree connection of a
teammate, so it's a full DM, no character limit.

**One sender only** — the teammate he already knows. Three people messaging the
same company reads as a mail merge.

> Hi Joe! [Name] here — we spoke on [street] yesterday afternoon, I'm the one
> who asked for your LinkedIn.
>
> Slightly mad update: we're at a hackathon this weekend and after that
> conversation we scrapped our idea and are building what you described instead.
>
> Two things you said stuck with us. That most of the roles you're hiring for
> have never existed at Legora before, so there's no playbook to hand anyone.
> And that nobody has time to look after new hires, so they have to be
> independent from day one. Put together, that means documentation can't fix it
> and a spare person can't fix it either.
>
> So we're building an agent that works out what a new role actually is from
> what the team is already doing, gives the new hire a real first task with the
> context to actually do it, and only pings a human when it genuinely can't
> unblock them.
>
> Two small asks:
>
> 1. Could we grab 15 minutes today? Any time — we're here all weekend. We'd
>    love to show you and make sure we're not building the wrong thing.
> 2. If not, one question you're uniquely placed to answer: is this a Legora
>    problem, or did you see the same thing at Lovable and Pleo?
>
> We demo Sunday morning. Would mean a lot to have your eyes on it before then.

**Get off LinkedIn immediately.** It's a dead channel on a weekend and the
deadline is Sunday 10:00. The single most important outcome of any reply is his
**WhatsApp number**. His email may also be visible under Contact info — take
both.

**The question worth asking him above all others:** *is this a Legora problem,
or did you see the same thing at Lovable and Pleo?* He is one of very few people
alive who can answer it, and if he says "it's the pattern", that is your market
validated by someone who has seen it four times.

Message the second contact too — separately, differently worded.
